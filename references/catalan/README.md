# Catalan reference material

Structured data extracted from beginner Catalan materials, used to check the Polish→Catalan course in `src/content/polish/catalan/` (age 13, level A1).

## Sources

| id | Document | Relevance | Extracted |
|---|---|---|---|
| `just-arrived` | *Just Arrived – Catalán para principiantes* (UPC SLT slides, 61 pp., Spanish explanations) | high | 39 phrases, 110 vocab, 8 grammar, 6 culture |
| `primeres-paraules` | *Les meves primeres paraules en català / Mis primeras palabras en español* (UPC SLT mentor guide, Dec 2019, 24 pp., Catalan and Spanish) | high | 74 phrases, 356 vocab, 13 grammar, 14 culture |
| `conversem` | *Conversem? Activitats de suport a l'aprenentatge de català inicial fora de l'aula* (J. Pujol, UPC and J. M. Boladeras, UdL, Sept 2016, 36 pp.) | high | 73 phrases, 115 vocab, 28 grammar, 5 culture |
| `catala-basic-2` | *Català Bàsic 2 – Materials per a les sessions presencials* (Maria Pardo, UPC SLT, Dec 2018, 32 pp., A2.1) | high | 35 phrases, 96 vocab, 5 grammar, 6 culture |
| `lexic-arquitectura` | *Lèxic bàsic d'arquitectura tècnica* (UPC SLT, 1996, 2nd ed., 35 pp.) | low | metadata plus 15 everyday words, 1 culture note |
| `petit-princep` | *El Petit Príncep* (Catalan translation of Saint-Exupéry, 41 pp.) | medium | word frequency only: 300 words |

Totals: 221 phrases, 692 vocab items, 54 grammar points, 32 culture notes, 23 topics and 300 frequency entries.

Notes on the sources:
- **Just Arrived**: the slides for days, numbers 1–100 and the alphabet are images. They were read from page renders.
- **Primeres paraules**: the guide has a Catalan half (pp. 3–13) and a Spanish half (pp. 14–24). Page numbers point to the Catalan half, and the `es` values come from the Spanish half.
- **Conversem**: uses the spelling before the 2017 reform (*sóc*, *dóna*, *véns*). Its phrases are copied as printed.
- **Català Bàsic 2**: the level is A2, so `compare.mjs` hides it by default. Pages 27–32 are a syllabus, so its grammar entries are summaries of that syllabus.
- **El Petit Príncep**: **copyrighted text – do not quote it in lessons.** Only single-word counts are stored, and no sentences or passages were copied.

## Files

- `catalan-reference.json`: the data, structured as `{ sources, topics, phrases, vocab, grammar, culture, frequency }`.
  - `page` is the PDF page number. `register` is `informal`, `formal` or `neutral`. `pl` translations were written by hand. Items read with doubt carry `"uncertain": true`, and some items have a `note`.
  - `vocab[].ca` holds the bare word, with its article or gender in `article_or_gender` (`m`, `f`, `m pl`, `adj`…). Paired forms are written `suec / sueca`.
  - `grammar[].detect` is an extra field: marker strings that `compare.mjs` looks for to decide whether the lessons cover that point.
  - `frequency[]` is `{ word, count, in_lessons }` and is generated by `build-frequency.mjs`.
- `compare.mjs`: gap report (Node, no dependencies).
- `build-frequency.mjs`: regenerates `frequency` from `text/el-petit-princep.txt` and recomputes `in_lessons`.
- `text/`: `pdftotext -layout` output. `source/`: the original PDFs.

## Usage

Run from the repo root:

```bash
node references/catalan/compare.mjs                 # gaps per topic + grammar not mentioned
node references/catalan/compare.mjs --topic food    # one topic, every missing phrase
node references/catalan/compare.mjs --source conversem
node references/catalan/compare.mjs --all           # include Català Bàsic 2, the glossary and proper names
node references/catalan/compare.mjs --frequency     # 50 most frequent Petit Príncep words not found in any lesson
node references/catalan/build-frequency.mjs         # rebuild the frequency list after lessons change
```

Matching is a plain contains-check on normalised text (lowercased, punctuation and hyphens turned into spaces, whole-word). Vocab counts as covered if any of its `/` variants appears in a lesson. A phrase is listed as missing only if none of its sentences appear; exchanges where only some sentences appear are counted as "partly covered".

Disagreements between the sources:
- *soc* (Just Arrived, Primeres paraules, lessons) vs *sóc* (Conversem, Petit Príncep).
- *Adeu* (Primeres paraules) vs *Adéu* (Conversem, lessons).
- Letter names: *eme, ene, erre, esse, ix* (Just Arrived) vs *ema, ena, erra, essa, ics* (Primeres paraules, Conversem).
- Numbers: *vint-i-un, trenta-un* (Just Arrived) vs *vint-i-u, trenta-u* (Conversem). The lessons teach both: *u* for counting and *un* before a noun.
